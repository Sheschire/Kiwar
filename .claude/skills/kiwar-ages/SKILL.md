# Kiwar ages and research (chantier 08)

Load `kiwar-sim` too (deterministic, exact offline), `kiwar-client` for the UI and models.
The Kikiwiwis (laboratory "against nature", infusions, titan works, obstacles) are the second
half of chantier 08: see the `kiwar-titans` skill. Their techs (`juiceInfusion`, `titanWorks`,
`greenBlood`) are the `unnatural` branch of `TECHS`.

## Data

- `data/ages.ts` — `AGE_IDS` (eden, stone, iron, industry), `MAX_AGE`.
- `data/research.ts` — `TECH_IDS`, `TECHS` (age, branch economy/defense/exploration/passage,
  cost in points, Library level, `effects`), `PASSAGE_TECHS[age]` (the tech that opens it),
  `RESEARCH` (Spirit bonus, bank per Library level), `RESEARCH_SCALE` (raw units per point).
  Adding a tech: id in `TECH_IDS` (zod enum: adding is fine), entry in `TECHS`, FR + EN in
  `techs.<id>.name/desc`; a new kind of effect goes into `TechEffects`, `techEffects` and the
  place it applies.
- `data/buildings.ts` — `library` (`research` = points per hour of an average researcher),
  `monument` (3 levels, Nest 3 / 6 / 9), `requiresAge` on levels. **Chantier 30: three levels
  per age** (Eden 1–3, Stone 4–6, Iron 7–9, Industry 10–12; level n needs age
  `ageOfLevel(n)` = ⌊(n−1)/3⌋ and the Nest n, the Nest its level − 1); levels 4–12 are
  generated by `withAgeLevels` from `AGE_GROWTH` (factors per level, `ageCost` / `ageStorage`
  and the `workerSlots` / `queueSize` / `trainingSlots` extras on the first level of an age;
  one rule per type: tune there, not level by level). The lab keeps 3 levels (Nest 7 / 7 / 10).
  Tech `libraryLevel`: 4 = Stone Library, 7 = Iron, 10 = Industry.
  `isManualWorkplace(type)`: idle kiwis never join it on their own (the Library). Since
  chantier 15 the automation (`fillResearch`, `kiwar-tasks`) fills it with the kiwis with the
  most Spirit while a technology is chosen, and sends them back after.
- Chantier 15: tech `meteorDrill` (Stone, economy) — a drill on every worked crater, × by age
  (`METEORS.drill`, `drillFactor`).

## Simulation (`sim/research.ts`)

- `ageOf(state)` = the monument's level (0 = Eden). Everything age-dependent reads it:
  `maxLevelNow`, upgrade checks (`ageTooLow`), `shipOf(state)`, `wavePower` (`THREATS.ageFactor`),
  `meteorStock` (`METEORS.ageFactor`), `techStatus` ('age').
- `researchPower(v)`, `researchPerHour(state)` (Library workers × presence × night × hunger ×
  morale × damaged; 0 in a shelter), `researchRate` = integer raw units per ms. Every factor
  must change only at events (same rule as `workforce`).
- `state.research` {points, current, done}: `accrueResearch` (into the current tech, or the bank
  up to `researchBank`), `nextResearchEvent`, `researchTick` (event `researchDone`). In
  `accrue`, research is accrued **before** `accrueKiwis` (level-ups change Spirit).
- `techEffects(state)` (memoised on `done`): harvest per resource (`harvestOf`), storage
  (`capacity`), turret defense (`defenseOf`), guards and firearms (`guardPower(v, state)`),
  travel (`travelTime(…, tech)`), rations (expeditions), build time (`constructionDuration`),
  injuries (`waveOutcome`), ship armour and recon (`shipOf`).
- Commands: `setResearch { tech | null }` (unknownTech, techDone, techAgeLocked,
  libraryTooLow; banked points may complete it at once), monument via `build`/`upgrade`
  (passageMissing, monumentExists; `notDemolishable`). Event `ageReached` on monument levels.
- Save v13; `isPlausible` → `plausibleResearch` and `plausibleAge` (`gameService.ts`).
- Tests: `test/research.test.ts` (bank, exact completion big vs small steps, Spirit, errors,
  effects, migration), `test/ages.test.ts` (levels 4–6 data, monument, level gates, threats ×
  age, import), `test/aliens.test.ts` (ships by age; `reliable()` adds an Iron-age monument).

## Client

- `ui/components/ResearchModal.tsx`: `ResearchSummary` (points, rate, progress, ETA), `AgeInfo`
  (age, next step, what it opens), `ResearchTab` (tree by age) inside `PlansModal` (chantier
  11: store `modal: 'plans'`, `plansTab: 'research'`, `BookIcon` button in `TopBar`; each
  `TechCard` lists `describeTech` before → after). `TilePanel`: Library and monument sheets, `needsAge` hint.
- Models (chantier 33, details in `kiwar-client`): **the look follows the building's level**
  (`lookOf`: levels 1–3 Eden, 4–6 Stone, 7–9 Iron, 10–12 Industry; tier = rank in the age);
  recipes in `render/models/` built from `ageKit.ts` (dry stones and thatch, half-timbering
  and tiles, brick, sheet metal and glass; tier 2 annex, tier 3 crown); `dress` recolours with
  `ageColor` (family hues stay) by the look's age. The colony's age only picks the docked ship
  (`buildingModel(type, level, shipAway, colonyAge)`); the Library's laboratory vat comes with
  its Iron levels. Models are cached per type:level:colonyAge; `BuildingsView` rebuilds
  meshes when the colony's age changes. `PathsView.sync(paths, age)`.
  `ship.ts` and the docked bird use `shipIdForAge(ageOf(state))`.
- Events: toasts `events.researchDone` / `events.ageReached`, sounds `research.done`,
  `age.reached`, fireworks over the monument (`GameRenderer.onGameEvents`).
- Screenshots: push techs into `controller.state.research.done` or set building levels on
  `controller.state` right before a `debugGrant` dispatch (see `kiwar-client`).

## Stardust (chantier 13, `kiwar-sky`)

- Branch `stars` (`clearNights`, `starWatch` at Stone; `cometLure`, `starIron` at Iron):
  `TechDef.stardust` is paid by `setResearch` when chosen, refunded when abandoned.
  `nightWork(state)` replaces `CYCLES.nightWork` (harvest and research).
- `LevelDef.stardust`: the monument's Iron (15) and Industry (25) levels; `upgradeLock` returns
  `notEnoughStardust`, `QueueItem.paidDust` is refunded on cancel. The Stone age needs none.

## Chantier 38 (skill `kiwar-forge`)

Tech `forge` (Stone, effect `forge`, opens the Forge and the stone gear); `smelting` / `armor`
open the iron gear, `firearms` / `machines` the steel. `LevelDef.iron` on levels 7+, the
monument (30 Iron, 60 Industry), the Iron levels of the camp and the Forge (`withIron`);
`TechDef.iron` (armor, machines, firearms). Never a level costing more iron than the cap of
the Nest it needs (test in `iron.test.ts`).

## Balance

- **Chantier 30**: targets Stone ~12 h, Iron ~3 days, Industry ~10 days (attentive), occasional
  × 1.5 (Industry × 1.25); `AGE_TARGETS_H` in `tools/balance/src/report.ts`. Eden techs are
  cheap (25–40 points) so the Stone passage is not stuck behind them; the Stone passage needs
  the Library 2 (research ~20 pts/h there, ~38 at Library 3, halved at night).

- Bot (`tools/balance/src/bot.ts`): `researchStaffing` (one researcher per five kiwis, frees a
  worker of the busiest producer), `ageDecision` (Library after Nest 3 + School, `TECH_ORDER`,
  Library upgrades for the next tech, monument with saving up), `upgradeOk` (age and Nest
  gates). Milestones `library`, `stone`, `iron`, `industry`.
- `pnpm balance:ages -- --seeds 3 --days 14 [--biomes a,b]`: median time to Nest 3, Library and
  each age per biome (~10 min for 2 seeds × 6 biomes × 12 days, one process per biome).
  Reference (end of the first half of chantier 08): Stone 34–41 h, Iron 88–104 h, Industry
  175–194 h for the active bot, biomes within ±6 %. Chantier 18: passages 1 000 / 2 900 /
  5 800 points for the complete bot with the chantier 15 automation (Stone 36 h, Iron 4.0 d, Industry 7.9 d); the long ages
  runs are now a section of `pnpm balance --ages 10` (both profiles). Tune passages (`TECHS` costs),
  monument costs/durations and `AGE_GROWTH`.
